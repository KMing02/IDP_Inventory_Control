## Incomplete historical evidence

Not all per-product/per-seed outputs are retained for the main FCNN arm, alternative KAN/window/MLP comparisons,
training-size comparisons, 3,000-product evaluation, or intermediate and negative development experiments.
Some results are historical constants in `report/results.py` and records in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`.
The final MonoKAN CSVs do not provide missing comparator rewards: they cannot independently reproduce every
alternative-design paired CI or p-value. This is a limit of the supplied material, not evidence that the original
experiments did not occur. Complete historical environments and configurations have not been reconstructed.

The historical expanding-window MLP control records `level_network: mlp` and `level_hidden_units: 40` in the
experiment index. In this implementation, two hidden layers of 40 produce 1,884 total parameters versus MonoKAN's
1,892: approximate parameter matching. Switching only the network type at the default width of eight is not that
control. The historical MLP and running-mean KAN controls also use different matching partners and must not be pooled.

## Interpretation and diagnostic limits

- Main paired tests average each product over training seeds, then compare products. Their CIs are not uncertainty
  intervals over unrestricted retraining seeds.
- Development and checkpoint selection used the reported validation products. The 3,000-product population contains
  the original 300 and is not an independent protected test.
- Main and alternative designs differ in more than the monotonic constraints. Their reward difference is not an
  isolated effect of the number of constrained inputs.
- A nonsignificant mean difference is not a formal demonstration of equivalence.
- Regret averages relative shortfalls only where the reference profit is positive (279 of the 300 products).
- The generic soft-quantile metadata retains old target-ratio and initialization descriptions. The generic target
  diagnostic includes an extra historical-mean factor; its magnitudes must not be treated as final target levels.
- The generic saved spline CSV is empty and does not certify the soft-quantile wrapper. Structural arguments require
  all signed paths, spline conditions, extrapolation and the decoder, not average curves or first-layer checks alone.
- `TESTED_ENVIRONMENT.md` records an observed local environment, not a complete lockfile or a clean-install guarantee.

This update changes no simulator, objective, architecture, normalization, initialization, experiment config or saved
metric. Two submission-only CLI conveniences (`--models` on the structural-audit wrapper and `--help` on the FCNN
training script) are not imported by this report/documentation sync. Research implementations otherwise match the
submitted package after normalizing line endings; a config-comment-only difference is also left untouched.
